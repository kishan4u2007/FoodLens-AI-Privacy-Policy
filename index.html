Privacy Policy

Developer: Tooligen Labs • Application: FoodLens AI (Package: in.kishanraikwar.foodlensai) • Effective Date: October 24, 2026
Privacy Commitment: FoodLens AI is built with privacy by design. We do not sell your personal data, track your location, or require you to create an account to scan and analyze food products. Your scanned history and saved items stay stored directly on your personal device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to FoodLens AI ("the Application"), developed and operated by Tooligen Labs ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using FoodLens AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

FoodLens AI does not require you to create an account or provide personal identifiers (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number) to access its core features.

  • On-Device Local Storage: When you scan barcodes, save favorite products, or adjust preferences (such as language selection), this information is saved locally on your device via Android's local preferences storage. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Product Lookups: When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the application queries public food databases (such as Open Food Facts) and verified brand indexes strictly to retrieve product nutritional facts, ingredients, and allergen data. No user identifier is attached to these queries.

3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To deliver its food transparency and scanner functionality, FoodLens AI requests access to specific device hardware:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively while you actively use the barcode scanner or label capture feature to read barcodes and nutrition facts labels. Video frames and photo captures are processed in real time on your device and are never uploaded to remote ad servers or third-party tracking services.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

FoodLens AI may interact with trusted third-party services to provide accurate food composition data and standard platform functionality:

  • Open Food Facts API: Used as an open, collaborative database for food product lookups. Their privacy policy is available at Open Food Facts Terms & Privacy.
  • Google Play Services: Used strictly for application distribution, secure delivery, and standard crash/stability telemetry provided automatically by the Android operating system.

5. Advertising and Monetization

FoodLens AI does not serve third-party banner ads, pop-up interstitials, or behavioral tracking advertisements. We do not participate in cross-app advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13. While the application provides kid-friendly food moderation metrics for parental guidance, use of the app by minors must be supervised by a parent or legal guardian.

7. Data Security

The security of your data is paramount to Tooligen Labs. Because we avoid storing your personal scans or history on remote central servers, your data is inherently protected by your personal device's native sandboxing and encryption mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app functionality or regulatory obligations. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We recommend reviewing this page periodically for any updates.
